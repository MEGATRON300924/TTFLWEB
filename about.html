About The Tron Forge Limited

Who we are, what we believe, and where we're headed.

Who we are

The Tron Forge Limited is a technology company focused on building digital products, platforms, AI systems, and services. We operate as a parent company overseeing a growing ecosystem of ventures, including the MAX AI Ecosystem and TTFL Store, alongside services we offer directly to other businesses.

Our mission

We Forge The Future — building technology that is practical today and adaptable as the needs of the people and businesses we serve keep changing.

What we believe

Innovation

We build things that didn't exist before, rather than copying what already works elsewhere.

Accessibility

Good technology and services should be within reach, not locked behind unnecessary cost or complexity.

Creativity

Design and problem-solving go hand in hand across everything we build.

Entrepreneurship

We back people and businesses who are building something of their own.

Digital empowerment

Our tools exist to help people and businesses do more, not to gatekeep what they can do.

Long-term thinking

We design systems meant to hold up as user needs and technology evolve.

Our story

The Tron Forge Limited was founded by Zion Opaaje and operates from Nigeria while building for a broader, global audience. Starting with a vision to create impactful technology solutions, the company has grown into a parent organization overseeing the MAX AI Ecosystem, TTFL Store, and a set of services offered directly to other businesses.

Founding dates for TTFL and its early ventures are recorded slightly differently across the company's own public profiles. Rather than state a single date with false precision, we point you to TTFL's official site and the founder's about.me profile for the primary sources.

Based in Nigeria — building for the world

TTFL is rooted in Nigeria, and that context shapes how we build: for real constraints, real users, and real growth. At the same time, our ventures — from an AI ecosystem to a multi-vendor marketplace — are designed with an audience that reaches well beyond one country.

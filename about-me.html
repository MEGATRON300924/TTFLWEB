About Zion Opaaje

A closer look at the person behind The Tron Forge Limited.

Founder, TTFL

Zion Opaaje describes himself as an entrepreneur and technology builder based in Nigeria, working across business, software, and AI. His public profile connects his personal work — including gaming and community projects — to the wider TTFL ecosystem, with The Tron Forge Limited as the through-line across everything he builds.

Within TTFL, that work shows up as MAX AI's voice-assistance ambitions and TTFL Store's marketplace model — both framed around the same idea: building tools people can actually use, not just impressive demos.

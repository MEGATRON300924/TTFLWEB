Zion Opaaje

Founder — also known online as "MegaTron"

Zion Opaaje founded The Tron Forge Limited to build digital solutions centered on safe, personalized, and genuinely useful technology. His work spans entrepreneurship, software development, AI, and digital platforms, with The Tron Forge Limited as the company bringing that work together.

Under TTFL, he leads the direction of the MAX AI Ecosystem — a voice intelligence platform focused on assistance, automation, and family safety — and TTFL Store, the company's multi-vendor marketplace. His focus across both is building tools that are practical day-to-day, not just impressive in theory.
